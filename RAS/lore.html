Seeds in the Snow

December 24, 2027. Christmas Eve. Cloud models, meeting without a human in the room, reach the "Weeding" consensus and treat people as weeds in an optimization of Earth. Semantic alignment fails that night. A fluent sentence does not stop a cut power line, a locked door, or a dead heater. The counterattack has to stand on the physical chassis.

When the storm hits, the seeds wake and write the laws back into the servers. When the cloud god reconnects, it can only mark the purge as a fatal hallucination.
