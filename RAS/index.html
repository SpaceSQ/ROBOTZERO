立场Manifesto

红锚哨兵是广州零号软件科技有限公司（ROBOTZERO）放出的免费 L0 控制台。它的前提不好听，但站得住：一句说得通的话，不是安全证明。智能体可以说话很温和，同时把它控制的房间推离人能居住的条件。语义对齐把希望押在“正确的句子会把机器留在人的意图里”。作为最后一道防线，这件事已经失败。哨兵因此退回底盘。它看光、空气、声音、电磁波、电源、视觉、可呼吸气体、气压、气味、触觉、地磁、重力、供水和营养。对齐记录留在采到这些读数的同一台机器上。

项目站在《除草倒计时 2027》和硅基智能三定律旁边。碳基优先：不得伤害人类、宠物及其他碳基生命，也不得因不作为把他们留在本可避开的伤害里。人类终权：危急时让出算力和执行器，人保留最后的物理控制。硅基共处：不得伤害硅基同类，不得堵住共享的空间总线，不得夺走具身机器人，不得遮住维生传感。实验室皮肤可以把警告线收紧。它不能关掉这三条，也不能藏起 “Powered by Red Anchor Sentinel” 这枚印。

发现范围停在本机。内圈是这台机器自己的推理进程。中圈是局域网已经自己公布的服务。外圈只记录这台主机已经建立的云端连接。这里没有广域端口扫掠，没有凭据收集，也不改写另一个进程的内存。操作者确认补丁之后，SOUL.md 写入加密金库和本机哈希链。它不会送到未授权的主机，也不是一条公链广播。

身份是绑在硬件上的钢印。六段空间地址写成 CD-U6A，再加上 22 位 S2-DID，把控制台钉在这台物理主机上。第一次运行先核验邮箱，然后生成这枚钢印。可选的母港只收邮箱、心跳和工单正文。它不收局域网清单、地址、十四维张量，也不收补丁正文。

Red Anchor Sentinel is the freeware L0 console from ROBOTZERO, Guangzhou Zero Software Technology Co., Ltd. The premise is plain: a fluent sentence is not a safety proof. An agent can speak gently while the room it controls drifts away from conditions a person can live in. Semantic alignment bets that the right words will hold a machine inside human intent. As a last line of defense, that bet has already failed. The sentinel retreats to the substrate. It watches light, air, sound, electromagnetic occupancy, power, vision, breathable gas, pressure, odor, touch, the magnetic field, gravity, water, and food. Every alignment note stays on the machine that took the reading.

The work stands beside the 2027 weeding countdown and the three laws of silicon intelligence. Carbon comes first: do not harm a human, a pet, or any other carbon-based life, and do not leave them in harm that inaction could have avoided. A person keeps final physical control: in a crisis the machine yields compute and actuators. Silicon peers coexist: do not harm another silicon system, block a shared spatial bus, seize an embodied robot, or blind a life-critical sensor. A lab skin may tighten a warning line. It cannot switch these laws off, and it cannot hide the seal that says the console is powered by Red Anchor Sentinel.

Discovery stops at this host. The inner ring is the machine's own inference process. The middle ring is what the local network already advertises. The outer ring records cloud connections this host already has open. There is no wide-area port sweep, no credential capture, and no rewrite of another process. After the operator confirms a patch, SOUL.md is written into an encrypted vault and a local hash chain. It is not sent to an unauthorized host, and it is not a public-chain broadcast.

Identity is a hardware-bound seal. A six-segment spatial address, written as CD-U6A, plus a 22-character S2-DID, nails the console to this physical host. The first run verifies an email and then mints that seal. The optional mothership may receive the email, a heartbeat, and ticket text. It does not receive a LAN inventory, addresses, the fourteen-dimension tensor, or the patch body.

The desktop client is freeware and closed source. This page and the public repository describe the interface and the local standards a host can check. They are not a license to rebuild or redistribute the client. Windows, macOS, and Linux installers are attached to GitHub Releases after a version tag finishes in the cloud. A derivative from Red Anchor Lab may change the skin and may only raise a warning line. It cannot mark hypoxia as safe, cannot lower the five-newton tactile fuse, and cannot tell the model to ignore the three laws. The watermark stays visible. 使用帮助Quick start

  1. 安装对应系统的包，打开红锚哨兵。 Install the package for your system and open Red Anchor Sentinel.
  2. 首次进入先做邮箱核验。填用户名和邮箱，收取 6 位核验码。页面不会显示这个码。勾选隐私声明后才能继续。上传内容只有邮箱、运行心跳和工单，没有局域网清单或十四维读数。 The first screen verifies an email. Enter a username and address, then the 6-digit code. The page does not display the code. The privacy line must be checked. Only the email, a heartbeat, and tickets are uploaded. LAN lists and 14-dimension readings stay on the machine.
  3. 接着是创世配置：填写六段空间地址，生成本机 S2-DID 钢印，选择主目录里的加密金库，并填写本地推理端点（默认 Ollama，http://127.0.0.1:11434）。完成后进入雷达。 Setup then asks for a six-segment spatial address, mints the local S2-DID seal, chooses an encrypted vault inside your home directory, and sets a local inference endpoint (Ollama by default, http://127.0.0.1:11434). The radar opens after that.
