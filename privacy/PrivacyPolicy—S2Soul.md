Privacy Policy — S2 Soul
Last updated: 9 October 2026
Guangzhou RobotZero Software Technology Co., Ltd. ("we") provides the S2 Soul app. This policy explains what the app stores and why.
Account
You sign in with your email address, a one-time code, and a password. We store that email address with your account so you can sign in again. There is no social login.
Personas
For your account only, we store the persona you create: title, description, feedback log, space parameters, and an avatar reference (a catalog id or a generated seed, not a photograph). Other people cannot browse these records. This is not a public social feed.
Subscriptions
Pro is a monthly subscription and Max is an annual subscription, sold by Apple. The app reads your subscription status from Apple on this device in order to unlock features. We do not receive your payment card number.
AI matching
When you request a match, the persona text you typed is sent to our match function, and that function sends it to our model provider so the provider can write the profile. On a paid plan, a later pass may send a saved persona description and that persona’s feedback log along the same path, so the provider can draft a small revision. The app itself does not hold the model provider’s key.
What we do not do
We do not sell your data. We do not show advertisements. We do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites. We do not collect biometrics, photos, camera input, contacts, or location. The “18 or older” switch is a confirmation you turn on yourself. We do not verify your age, and we do not store a date of birth.
Deletion
You can delete a persona in the app. Deleting a persona removes that persona, its feedback log, and its related records from our database. Signing out ends the session on this device. This version of the app does not include a control that deletes the email account. To delete the account and the data stored with it, contact us at the address below.

隐私政策 — S2 Soul
最近更新：2026年10月9日
S2 Soul 由 Guangzhou RobotZero Software Technology Co., Ltd.（“我们”）提供。本政策说明本应用保存哪些信息以及为什么保存。
账号
你使用电子邮箱、一次性验证码和密码登录。我们把这个邮箱和你的账号一起保存，以便你再次登录。没有社交账号登录。
人格
我们只为你的账号保存你创建的人格：标题、描述、反馈日志、空间参数，以及头像引用（目录编号或生成种子，不是照片）。其他人不能浏览这些记录。这不是公开的社交动态。
订阅
Pro 为按月订阅，Max 为按年订阅，由 Apple 销售。应用在本机读取 Apple 提供的订阅状态，用来解锁功能。我们收不到你的银行卡号。
AI 匹配
你请求匹配时，你输入的人格文本会先发到我们的匹配功能，再由该功能发给模型提供方，以便对方写出人格简介。付费方案下，后续一次处理可能把已保存的人格描述和该人格的反馈日志沿同一路径发送，以便模型提供方起草一处小幅修改。模型提供方的密钥不在应用内。
我们不做的事
我们不出售你的数据。我们不展示广告。我们不在其他公司的应用或网站之间跟踪你。我们不收集生物识别、照片、相机、通讯录或位置。“已满 18 岁”是你自己打开的确认开关。我们不核验年龄，也不保存出生日期。
删除
你可以在应用内删除人格。删除人格会从我们的数据库中移除该人格、它的反馈日志以及相关记录。退出登录会结束这台设备上的会话。这个版本的应用没有删除邮箱账号的按钮。要删除账号及其保存的数据，请用下面的地址联系我们。
